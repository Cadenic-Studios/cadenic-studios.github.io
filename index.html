Privacy & Compliance

LAST UPDATED: JUNE 2026

Cadenic Studios develops and operates mobile software applications, including Delta-V and Scorebug. We are committed to protecting user privacy across all device operations.

1. Local Device Telemetry & Balances

Cadenic Studios software installations preserve progression metrics locally on user hardware. System assets, balances, stardust, and garage configuration logs are isolated exclusively within standard sandboxed device storage.

2. Device Network Permissions & Partners

Our products implement features that access anonymous advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID) and device identifiers via Google AdMob, and handle transaction tokens via RevenueCat APIs, in order to process monetization actions securely.

3. Contact

For any privacy request or data enquiry, contact Cadenic Studios directly and we will respond promptly.
